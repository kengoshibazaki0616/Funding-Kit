FAQ

Common questions

How much can I access?
Typically $10,000 to $150,000, depending on your credit and business setup. The calculator gives a realistic range based on what you tell it — nothing is confirmed until a lender actually approves it.
How long does it take?
There are two clocks, and it is worth separating them. Once your file is ready, the application round itself usually runs a couple of weeks. Getting the file ready is the part that varies: if your credit is already clean, that step is nothing; a repair runs a few months of dispute rounds; building a US credit file from scratch realistically takes about a year. We will tell you which one you are on at the first call, before you spend anything.
Do I need collateral or perfect credit?
No collateral is required. Stronger credit helps, but structure and sequencing matter just as much, and we will tell you honestly where you stand.
Can you guarantee I’ll get funded, or that my score will go up?
No — and legally, nobody can. Under the Credit Repair Organizations Act it is against the law for a company like ours to promise a specific score increase or that a particular item will come off your report. So if someone is promising you that, they are telling you something they are not allowed to say. What we will do is give you a straight read on where you stand before you spend anything, and tell you plainly when we are not the right fit.
I moved to the US and have no credit history here. Can you help?
Usually yes, but as a build rather than a repair. What matters is whether you have a Social Security number, whether you live in the US, and how deep your US credit file is — your visa type is not a factor.
