# Funding Kit LLC — Consulting & Funding Services Agreement

**Prepared for (Client):** _______________________________________
**Provided by:** Funding Kit LLC, 3345 162nd St, Flushing, NY 11358 (the "Company")

This Consulting & Funding Services Agreement (the "Agreement") governs the relationship between **Funding Kit LLC** (the "Company" or "Consultant") and the undersigned **Client**.

**WHEREAS,** the Company assists clients in obtaining business credit cards and other lending products, including optimizing the client's credit profile and preparing and submitting applications to banking/lending institutions. The Company uses its best efforts and all reasonable avenues to secure funding through its standard practices. Approval of any funding is at the **sole discretion of the lender**; the Company does not guarantee any result.

**Credit products covered:** 0% interest business credit cards, business lines of credit, and (where applicable) personal loans and 0% interest personal credit cards. Before any application is submitted, the Client confirms which category of products they want.

---

## 1. Services
The Company will provide its expertise in obtaining consumer and business credit (the "Services"), including: credit-profile review and optimization guidance, application strategy and sequencing, lender/banker facilitation, and **preparation and submission of credit applications on the Client's behalf.**

## 2. Applications Completed by the Company
The Client engages the Company on a **done-for-you** basis: the Company will **prepare and submit credit applications on the Client's behalf** using information the Client provides.
- The Client will **review and approve each application before it is submitted.**
- The Client is **solely responsible for the accuracy of all information** provided for any application. The Company does not direct the Client to misstate income, revenue, or any other fact; all information submitted must be true and accurate.
- The Client — not the Company — **uses, and is solely responsible for repaying,** any credit obtained.

## 3. No Guarantee
There is no guarantee of approval for any application or that any lender will issue a commitment. The Company is not an employee of any financial institution and cannot make commitments on a lender's behalf. APRs and credit limits are determined by each lender's own underwriting.

## 4. Term
This Agreement begins on ____________. Either Party may terminate for any reason with **2 days' written notice.** The Client agrees to pay the success fee for any approvals obtained with the Company's assistance, even after termination. The Client owes nothing for approvals the Client obtains entirely on their own without the Company's assistance.

## 5. Payment Terms
- The Client will be invoiced **10% of the total approved funding amount, after each product approval** (i.e., only after the Service for that product has been performed).
- **Late fee:** balances unpaid more than 10 days after invoice will incur a **one-time late charge of 5% of the overdue balance, plus 2% per month** thereafter (or the maximum permitted by New York law, whichever is lower).
- **Payment method on file:** The Client authorizes the Company to charge the payment method on file for fees due under this Agreement (the 10% success fee, invoiced after each approval). The Company will notify the Client at least **3 business days** before charging any amount and will charge only amounts actually owed under this Agreement. The Client may update the payment method or dispute a charge by contacting the Company.
- If a balance remains overdue, the Company may pursue collection; the Client agrees to reasonable collection/legal costs **not to exceed 25%** of the balance owed.
- **Deposit:** *[Optional — Company currently operates pay-after-approval with no deposit. If a deposit is later used, attorney to confirm CROA/GBL 28-BB compliance and refund terms.]*

## 6. Accuracy of Information
The Client represents that all information provided to the Company and to any lender is **true, accurate, and complete.** The Company relies on the Client's information and is not responsible for consequences arising from information the Client provides that is inaccurate or incomplete.

## 7. Authorization
By signing, the Client authorizes the Company to:
1. Obtain the Client's credit and financial history from credit reporting agencies **to assess creditworthiness** (with the Client's signed FCRA consent below);
2. Review account statements related to the Client's credit lines;
3. **Prepare and submit credit applications on the Client's behalf**, using information the Client provides and approves;
4. Forward the Client's information to the Company's vetted partners/affiliates **solely to facilitate the Client's applications**;
5. Take acts reasonably incidental to the foregoing to assist the Client in obtaining credit.

The Client — not the Company — **uses and is responsible for** any credit card, line, or loan obtained. This authorization does **not** grant the Company the right to use the Client's credit.

**FCRA Consent:** The Client provides signed consent for any business or personal credit inquiries needed to perform the Services. No hard inquiry will be initiated without the Client's consent.

## 8. Confidentiality
The Company will not disclose the Client's confidential information (business plans, customer information, pricing) except as needed to perform the Services or as authorized by the Client.

## 9. Intellectual Property
The Client holds all rights in any work product from the Services. The Company will not claim ownership.

## 10. Indemnification
The Client agrees to indemnify and hold the Company harmless from claims and reasonable legal costs arising from the Client's business or the Client's own acts/omissions, including the Client's default on any credit facility. The Client's obligations on any credit facility obtained are solely the Client's responsibility.

## 11. Personal Guarantee
If the Client is an LLC, corporation, LLP, or other entity, the individual(s) signing personally guarantee, jointly and severally, payment of all fees and costs under this Agreement.

## 12. Signature Authority
The undersigned attests they are an Officer, Director, or Member with at least 25% ownership of the entity and have authority to bind it.

## 13. No Oral Modification
No modification is valid unless in writing signed by both Parties.

## 14. Governing Law & Venue
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of New York**, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction and venue of the state and federal courts located in **Queens County, New York**, and waive trial by jury.

**You may cancel this Agreement, without penalty or obligation, within THREE (3) BUSINESS DAYS from the date you sign it.** To cancel, mail or deliver a signed, dated notice to Funding Kit LLC, 3345 162nd St, Flushing, NY 11358, before midnight of the third business day after signing. If you cancel, any payments made will be returned within 10 days of the Company's receipt of your notice.
